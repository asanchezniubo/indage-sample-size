# Data sources

- `table1_2026_proposal.csv` — Table 1 of this proposal: the target sample of 1,000 adults by autonomous community and five-year age band. Regional totals are proportional to `bedpd2023_certified_disability_by_ccaa.csv`, with a floor of 15 participants per community; the age profile is the one adopted in `R/00_assumptions.R` and compared in `R/05_allocation.R`. Rounding is by largest remainders within each region, so the column totals differ by a few units from the exact age profile.
- `table1_2025_proposal.csv` — Table 1 of the 2025 proposal (sample of 1,042 by autonomous community and five-year age band), transcribed from the submitted proposal.
- `bedpd2023_certified_disability_by_ccaa.csv` — people with a certified disability degree of 33% or higher by autonomous community, all deficiency types. Source: IMSERSO (2025), *Base Estatal de Datos de Personas con Valoración del Grado de Discapacidad. Informe a 31/12/2023*, section 2.1. Asturias, Ceuta and Melilla report 2022 figures.
- `bedpd2023_id_age_strata.csv` — people aged 35 or over whose main deficiency is intellectual or developmental, with a degree of 33% or higher, by age stratum. Same source, section 3.5.1 (p. 30). The frame gives no breakdown of this category by autonomous community, which is why the regional allocation uses the distribution of the whole certified-disability population instead; the regional proportions carry no inferential weight, because autonomous community enters the analyses as a clustering level and as a covariate, not as a weight.
